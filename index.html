What's assumed here, so you can check it.
  1. Plan prices, extra-user prices and add-on prices are the published USD West figures. Per-message rates come from the Wati usage rate card that applies to this region, effective 1 July 2026, and are charged on the recipient's country. Each plan tier has its own rates — the higher tiers are cheaper per message.
  2. Message rates are quoted in USD on the rate card. Where the plan is billed in another currency, the two are converted into your chosen output currency at today's rate, shown under question 8. Service messages have their own per-country rate, lower than the utility rate.
  3. The rate cards this calculator is built from are linked under question 1, so you can open the source document on a call rather than quoting from memory.
  4. The estimate picks the cheapest plan that meets your requirements, but you can pin a specific plan in the receipt to quote a higher tier. If a pinned plan can't cover the requirements entered, the costs it can't carry are left out of the total and flagged above it.
  5. Growth's 15,000-broadcast monthly cap is checked against marketing plus utility volume. Authentication messages are API-triggered one-to-one and aren't treated as broadcasts. Pro and Business have no broadcast cap.
  6. Included automation triggers: 1,000 on Growth, 2,000 on Pro, 5,000 on Business. Top-ups are sold per 1,000 and the estimate rounds up to whole blocks. Unused triggers don't roll over.
  7. Included AI Co-pilot credits: 250 on Growth, 500 on Pro, 1,500 on Business. Top-ups are sold per 1,000.
  8. Extra WhatsApp numbers need the Business plan. Shopify is billed in USD at $4.99 a month everywhere, so it moves with the exchange rate in other currencies.
  9. Message charges are drawn from a prepaid wallet, separate from the subscription invoice.
  10. Business volume discounts above roughly a million messages a month aren't modelled. Also excluded: Astra AI agents, dedicated onboarding, technical account management, professional services, green-tick verification and the Blitz high-throughput add-on — all quoted separately.
  11. Exchange rates come from Frankfurter, falling back to Exchange Rate API. Both publish once a day, so the rate is a daily reference rate, not a dealing rate. Wati invoices in USD; your bank's rate on the day will differ slightly.
